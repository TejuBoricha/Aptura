Aptura is an elegant, offline-first video player engineered for privacy, speed, and smooth Material 3 aesthetics.

🔒 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Aptura does not request the internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET), so it cannot go online. Your files, watch history, bookmarks, and settings stay on your device.
• Zero Analytics, Telemetry, or Crash SDKs
• Zero Third-Party Tracking or Ads
• Zero Account Creation Required
• Scoped Storage Safe (Standard MediaStore & user-selected SAF folders only)

⚡ PLAYBACK ON YOUR PHONE'S OWN DECODERS
Built on the Android Media3 player:
• Uses your phone's hardware decoders, so what plays depends on your device. A file it cannot play is explained, with a way to open it in another app.
• Playback speed from 0.25x to 2.0x, and hold to boost to 2x.
• A-B repeat and frame-by-frame stepping.
• Picture-in-Picture (PiP) support.

🎨 BESPOKE MATERIAL 3 DESIGN
Immerse yourself in clean dark elegance:
• A true-black dark theme for OLED screens, and a light theme.
• Six accent colours: Cyan, Blue, Violet, Amber, Rose and Gold.
• Fluid, gesture-driven HUD: swipe for volume, brightness, and horizontal seeking.

⏱️ INTELLIGENT SLEEP TIMER
Fall asleep to your favorite shows without waking up to a dead battery:
• Sleep timer from 1 to 720 minutes with a gradual, 10-second volume fade-out.
• "At the end of this video" automatic pause option.
• The countdown keeps running while the app is in the background.

🎧 ADVANCED AUDIO & SUBTITLES
• Multi-track audio selection, and an audio sync offset for Bluetooth headphones.
• Subtitles from a file next to the video or one you import (.srt, .vtt, .ass and more), with adjustable timing, size and style.

📁 EFFICIENT IN-APP MEDIA MANAGEMENT
• Organize, move, copy, and rename videos directly within your storage.
• Direct folder-level paste and folder destination selection.
• Fast search, filtering, and customizable Grid / List layouts.
